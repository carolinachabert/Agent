Lighting & camera angles — reference, not generated

General one-time tips. This is physical, hands-on setup — it's Karolina's execution every time, not a repeating automated step.

LightingSoft, diffused daylight near a window. Avoid direct overhead light — it throws hard shadows across stones and casts glare on polished metal.
Jewellery angleTilt the piece slightly rather than flat-on, so facets catch light instead of reflecting the camera or ceiling.
Talking-head framingCamera at eye or chest height, not looking up or down. Keep the bench or boutique visible in the background for context.
StabilityA small tripod or propped phone beats handheld for anything with fine detail — even slight shake reads badly on close-up jewellery shots.
